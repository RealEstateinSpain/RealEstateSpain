# Real Estate in Spain — Premium Google Sites Redesign

## Purpose
This package is a complete design and content blueprint for rebuilding the existing Google Site as a premium Spanish real-estate / construction / investment website while staying within Google Sites' visual restrictions.

## Brand direction
- Primary: Deep Navy #0B1F33
- Secondary: Navy #122D46
- Accent: Warm Champagne Gold #C9A66B
- Background: Ivory #F7F5F0
- Body text: Slate #253444
- Headings: Georgia / elegant serif
- Body: Arial / clean sans-serif
- Visual style: Mediterranean luxury, understated, architectural, international

## Google Sites implementation
- Use a wide hero image as the section background.
- Use a dark transparent overlay effect by selecting a dark section background where available; otherwise choose a naturally darker image.
- Keep the first headline short: “Exceptional Property. Trusted Expertise. Spain.”
- Use two buttons in the hero: “Explore Properties” and “Private Consultation”.
- Use 4-column service blocks on desktop; Google Sites will stack them on mobile.
- Use image + text split sections for major service/property sections.
- Use an image carousel only where several actual properties are available; do not fill the carousel with generic stock images.
- Keep Arabic as a secondary line or dedicated Arabic page rather than mixing long English and Arabic paragraphs everywhere.
- Use the footer for legal notice, privacy policy, cookies and contact details.

## Important placeholders
Before publishing, replace:
- YOUR-EMAIL@example.com
- YOURNUMBER
- Spain address
- Actual operating locations
- Actual legal/company name
- Privacy/legal pages
- Any claim about “high-yield” or investment returns unless you can substantiate it

## What was changed from the current site
The original homepage was text-heavy and placed long English and Arabic paragraphs one after another. The redesign converts the content into shorter, scannable sections, introduces a premium visual hierarchy, creates distinct service categories, adds a buyer journey/process, and uses stronger calls to action.

The original site currently states that it offers premium real estate, construction/development and renovation/maintenance services and targets international clients. Those core themes are retained, but the wording has been made more restrained and professional.
